Commercial General Contractor for Southeast Michigan Businesses

RBF Construction builds and renovates commercial properties of all kinds for owners, developers and national brands across Genesee County, Oakland County and Southeast Michigan — office buildings, restaurants, medical and dental offices, retail stores, multi-tenant buildings, and government and institutional buildings. Our work includes projects for multiple Taco Bell and Subway locations, Jimmy John’s, Pizza Hut, Smoothie King, YaYa’s Chicken, Dollar Tree, Biggby Coffee, Cottage Inn Pizza and Athletico Physical Therapy, and a remodel for NBC25.

FAQ

Common Questions

What types of commercial projects does RBF build?

Office buildings and tenant build-outs, restaurants and drive-thrus, medical, dental and physical therapy offices, retail stores and multi-tenant buildings, libraries, nonprofit and government buildings, commercial renovations, facility upgrades such as pump stations, and the site work that goes with them.

Can you handle the site work and the building on a commercial project?

Yes. RBF has an in-house site development division for demolition, excavation, grading, utilities, paving and concrete, and we serve as the general contractor for the building.

Have you built for national brands?

Yes. Our work includes projects for national restaurant, retail and healthcare brands, as well as local business owners and developers.

Do you build commercial projects in Oakland County?

Yes. We build throughout Oakland County, including Troy, Southfield, Auburn Hills, Novi, Rochester Hills and Waterford, and across Southeast Michigan.
