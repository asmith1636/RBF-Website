Pole Barns, Garages & Post-Frame Buildings in Genesee County

Pole barns, detached garages, workshops and storage buildings — with the site prep, gravel base and concrete floor done by our own crews.

Pole Barns Built on a Proper Foundation

A pole barn is only as good as the ground it sits on. Because RBF Construction has its own excavation and site work division, we handle everything from clearing and grading the building site to the gravel base, concrete floor, apron and driveway — then build the post-frame structure itself.

Pole Barn and Post-Frame Projects

  • Detached garages
  • Workshops & hobby barns
  • RV, boat & equipment storage
  • Horse & hobby-farm barns
  • Agricultural buildings
  • Commercial storage buildings
  • Lean-tos & additions
  • Barn-style homes & living quarters

Options and Upgrades

  • Concrete floors and aprons, poured by our own crews
  • Overhead and walk doors sized for trucks, RVs, boats or equipment
  • Insulation, liner panels and heat for a year-round shop
  • Electrical service and lighting, including trenching from the house
  • Wainscot, windows, cupolas and color choices to match your home
  • Driveways and grading so water drains away from the building

Permits, Zoning and Site Planning

Most Michigan townships regulate accessory buildings with size limits, height limits and setbacks, and require a building permit. We review your township’s rules, plan the building location, pull permits and schedule inspections.

Our Pole Barn Process

  1. Site visit: we look at the location, access, drainage and how you’ll use the building.
  2. Design & estimate: size, doors, options and a clear price.
  3. Permits & site prep: clearing, grading and a compacted base.
  4. Construction: posts, trusses, steel, doors and options.
  5. Concrete & finish: floor, apron and final grading.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a permit to build a pole barn in Michigan?

In most Michigan townships, yes. Accessory buildings usually need a building permit and must meet zoning rules for size, height and setbacks. RBF reviews your township's requirements and pulls the permits.

Do you pour the concrete floor for pole barns?

Yes. Our own crews prepare the site, build the compacted base and pour the concrete floor and apron, so the building sits on a solid, properly drained foundation.

Can a pole barn be used as a heated workshop?

Yes. With insulation, interior liner panels, electrical service and a heat source, a pole barn makes an excellent year-round workshop or garage.

Where do you build pole barns?

Throughout Genesee County, Oakland County, Lapeer County, Livingston County and the rest of Southeast Michigan.
