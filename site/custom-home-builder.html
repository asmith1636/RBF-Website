Custom Home Builder for Flint, Genesee & Oakland County

Ground-up residential construction and additions — with our own crews handling the land clearing, excavation, grading and utilities.

Ground-Up Homes, Built by a Site Work Contractor

Most home builders subcontract everything below grade. RBF Construction does it ourselves. Our site development division handles clearing, excavation, grading, drainage, driveways and utility connections — the work that determines whether a house stays dry, level and problem-free for decades.

  • Custom single-family homes
  • Home additions
  • Garages & accessory buildings
  • Lot clearing & excavation
  • Foundations & basements
  • Driveways & flatwork
  • Water, sewer & utility connections
  • Design-build or your plans

Building on Rural, Wooded and Lake Lots

Much of northern and western Oakland County, Livingston County, Lapeer County and southern Genesee County is rural or wooded land with lakes, slopes and poor soils. These lots need careful site planning: tree removal, soil management, grading for drainage, long driveways and utility runs. That’s exactly where an in-house earthwork crew pays off.

From Plans to Keys

  1. Lot evaluation: we walk the property and identify site work, drainage and utility needs early.
  2. Design & budget: build from your architect’s plans or use our design-build approach.
  3. Permits & site work: clearing, excavation, foundation and utilities.
  4. Construction: framing through finishes with personal on-site supervision.
  5. Final inspections & walkthrough.

We build in Oakland, Livingston, Genesee and Lapeer counties and across Southeast Michigan. Tell us about your lot and plans.
