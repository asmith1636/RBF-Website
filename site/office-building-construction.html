Commercial Build-Outs & Office Construction

New professional office buildings, tenant improvements and office renovations across Genesee County, Oakland County and Southeast Michigan.

Office Space That Works for Your Business

RBF Construction builds new office buildings and renovates existing ones for owners, developers and tenants. Whether it’s a single-story professional office, a multi-tenant building or a build-out of leased space, we manage budget, schedule and quality from preconstruction to move-in.

  • Ground-up office buildings
  • Tenant improvements & build-outs
  • Office renovations & modernization
  • Multi-tenant professional buildings
  • Occupied-building phasing
  • Site work, parking & utilities

Tenant Build-Outs on Schedule

Lease start dates don’t move. We plan tenant improvement projects around your lease, coordinate with building management, and sequence demolition, framing, mechanical, electrical, data and finishes so you can open on time.

Renovating While You Stay Open

Need to update an office you’re working in? We phase work to keep your business operating — after-hours work where needed, dust and noise control, and clear communication about what’s happening each day.

Ground-Up With Site Work Included

For new buildings, our site development division handles excavation, utilities, parking lots and concrete, so the building and the site are managed by one team.

Serving office corridors in Troy, Southfield, Auburn Hills and Novi, Flint and Grand Blanc, and across Southeast Michigan. Request an office construction bid.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you do tenant build-outs?

Yes. RBF Construction builds out leased office, medical, retail and restaurant space, coordinating with building management and planning the work around your lease start date.

Can you renovate our office while we keep working?

Yes. We phase renovation work, schedule disruptive tasks after hours when needed, and control dust and noise so your business can keep operating.
