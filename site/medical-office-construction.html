Medical, Dental & Clinic Construction

New medical office buildings and build-outs for physicians, dentists, physical therapy, urgent care and specialty clinics.

Healthcare Spaces Built With Care

Medical construction has requirements other commercial projects don’t: accessibility, infection-control-friendly finishes, plumbing at nearly every exam room, privacy and sound control, and coordination with specialty equipment vendors. RBF Construction has built space for healthcare tenants including Athletico Physical Therapy (pictured above), and we bring that experience to every medical project.

  • Medical office buildings
  • Physician & specialty practices
  • Dental offices
  • Physical therapy clinics
  • Urgent care & walk-in clinics
  • Medical tenant build-outs
  • Veterinary clinics
  • Renovations & expansions

What Sets Medical Projects Apart

  • Accessibility: barrier-free entrances, restrooms, door hardware and clearances.
  • Plumbing-heavy layouts: sinks in exam and treatment rooms, sterilization areas and specialized drains.
  • Durable, cleanable finishes: materials selected for frequent cleaning.
  • Privacy: sound-attenuating walls and thoughtful layouts.
  • Equipment coordination: we coordinate with dental, imaging and equipment vendors on power, blocking, plumbing and any shielding their equipment requires.

We plan around your lease, licensing and equipment delivery so you can start seeing patients when planned.
