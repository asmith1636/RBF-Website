Residential Construction With Personal Supervision

RBF Construction brings the discipline of a commercial contractor to homes across Genesee County, Oakland County and Southeast Michigan. Whether you’re building a new home from the ground up, adding on, remodeling a kitchen or bathroom, or putting up a pole barn, you get one accountable contractor, personal on-site supervision and a trusted network of skilled tradesmen.

Built Right From the Ground Up

Many residential headaches start below grade or behind the walls. Because RBF runs its own site development and earthwork division, we handle excavation, grading, drainage and utility work with the same team that manages your build — so nothing falls through the cracks between contractors.

How We Work With Homeowners

  • Tailored solutions: we listen first, then scope the work to your needs and budget.
  • Clear budgeting and scheduling: we plan the work in detail and order long-lead items before demolition.
  • Permits and inspections handled: we pull permits and schedule inspections.
  • Clean, respectful job sites: dust control and daily cleanup in occupied homes.
