Kitchen Remodeling in Flint, Genesee & Oakland County

From a cabinet-and-countertop refresh to a full gut with a new layout — one contractor managing design, trades, permits and schedule.

A Kitchen Remodel Managed Like a Construction Project

Kitchens are the most complex room in a house: plumbing, electrical, ventilation, structure, cabinetry and finishes all have to come together in the right order. RBF Construction manages your kitchen remodel the same way we manage commercial projects — with a clear scope, a realistic schedule, skilled trades and personal on-site supervision.

What’s Included

  • Layout changes & wall removal
  • Cabinets & islands
  • Countertops & backsplashes
  • Tile, flooring & trim
  • Lighting & electrical upgrades
  • Plumbing & fixture relocation
  • Range hood ventilation
  • Permits & inspections

Opening Up Your Kitchen

Many Southeast Michigan homeowners want to open the kitchen to the living space. When a wall is load-bearing, we coordinate the engineering and install the beam and supports properly — then patch, finish and blend the floors and ceilings so it looks like it was always that way.

Our Kitchen Remodeling Process

  1. Consultation: we visit your home, measure, and talk through how you use the kitchen and your budget.
  2. Design & selections: layout, cabinets, counters, fixtures and finishes, with pricing tied to your choices.
  3. Permits & ordering: we pull required permits and order long-lead items like cabinets before demolition starts, so your kitchen is out of service for as little time as possible.
  4. Construction: demolition, rough-ins, inspections, installation and finishes, with dust control and daily cleanup.
  5. Walkthrough: a final punch list so every detail is right.

We remodel kitchens throughout Oakland County, Genesee County and the rest of Southeast Michigan. Pair your kitchen with a bathroom remodel to save on mobilization, or request a kitchen remodeling estimate.

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on scope. A cosmetic update with new counters and fixtures is much quicker than a full remodel that moves walls, plumbing and electrical. We provide a project schedule with your estimate and order cabinets and other long-lead items before demolition to minimize the time your kitchen is out of service.

Do I need a permit to remodel my kitchen in Michigan?

Most Michigan municipalities require permits when a remodel changes plumbing, electrical, mechanical or structural elements. RBF Construction handles permit applications and schedules the required inspections.

Can you remove a wall to open up my kitchen?

Yes. If the wall is load-bearing, we coordinate the engineering and install a properly sized beam and supports, then finish the floors and ceilings to blend in.

Do you remodel kitchens in Oakland County?

Yes. We remodel kitchens throughout Oakland County, including Troy, Rochester Hills, Clarkston, Waterford, Novi, Birmingham, Bloomfield Hills and Holly, as well as across Genesee County and Southeast Michigan.
