FAQ

Questions About Working With RBF

Quick answers about our services, service area and how to get a bid.

What does RBF Construction do?

RBF Construction, Inc. is a full-service general contractor serving Oakland County, Genesee County and Southeast Michigan. We do kitchen and bathroom remodeling, custom homes and additions, commercial construction of office buildings, restaurants, medical and dental offices and retail, plus complete site work: excavation, grading, underground utilities, parking lots, concrete and demolition.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Oakland County and Southeast Michigan, including Genesee, Livingston, Macomb, Wayne, Lapeer and Washtenaw counties. See all service areas.

Where is RBF Construction located?

Our office is at 1411 W Hill Rd, Flint, MI 48507, just off I-75 north of Oakland County. We’re open Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Call 810-938-8498 or email info@rbfconstruction.com.

Do you do kitchen and bathroom remodels?

Yes. We remodel kitchens and bathrooms throughout Oakland County and Southeast Michigan, from cabinet and countertop updates to full remodels with layout changes, walk-in showers and accessible, aging-in-place bathrooms. We handle the trades, permits and inspections.

What types of commercial buildings do you build?

Office buildings and tenant build-outs, restaurants including drive-thrus, medical, dental and physical therapy offices, retail stores and multi-tenant buildings. We’ve built for brands such as Jimmy John’s, Pizza Hut, Smoothie King, Taco Bell, Dollar Tree, Biggby Coffee, Cottage Inn Pizza and Athletico Physical Therapy.

Can RBF take a project from raw land to a finished building?

Yes. Our in-house site development division handles demolition, excavation, grading, utilities, paving and concrete, and we build the building too — one contractor from groundbreaking to completion.

What’s the difference between design-build and design-bid-build?

In design-build, one team handles design and construction under a single contract, which simplifies communication and can shorten schedules. In design-bid-build, an architect completes the design first and contractors bid on it. RBF does both. Read our design-build vs. design-bid-build guide.

How do I get a bid or estimate?

Call 810-938-8498 or send project details through our bid request form. Plans, photos, the site address and your target timeline help us respond faster.

I’m a subcontractor. How do I get on your bid list?

Submit your company information on our subcontractor bidding list page and we’ll reach out when projects in your trade and area come up for bid.
