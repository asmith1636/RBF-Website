Demolition & Site Clearing in Flint & Genesee County

Building demolition, interior selective demolition, pavement and foundation removal — clearing the way for what’s next.

Safe, Efficient Demolition

Whether you’re clearing a site for redevelopment or gutting an interior for a remodel, demolition sets the pace for the rest of the project. RBF Construction plans demolition carefully — utility disconnects, permits, protection of adjacent property and proper disposal — then moves quickly.

  • Commercial building demolition
  • Residential demolition
  • Interior selective demolition
  • Pavement & curb removal
  • Foundation removal
  • Site clearing
  • Debris hauling & disposal
  • Backfill & grading

Demolition as Step One

Because RBF is also a general contractor and site developer, demolition flows straight into excavation, utilities and new construction without waiting on another contractor.
